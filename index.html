Academic / career interests

Where intelligence meets a body

My long-term goal is to become an AI engineer and eventually build a humanoid robot — not just a research prototype, but a system that can perceive, reason, and act in everyday environments. I'm especially interested in machine learning and neural networks for perception and decision-making, reinforcement learning for physical control, and computer vision for spatial awareness. I'm also curious about the hardware side — actuators, sensors, and the trade-offs that come with putting intelligence into a physical body.
